How your author-date citations should be laid out

In the text

  • (Smith, 2020) — author and year in brackets
  • (Ip et al., 2025) — “et al.” is fine
  • (Smith & Jones, 2020) — two authors
  • (Smith, 2020; Jones, 2021) — separate several with a semicolon
  • Kraft et al. (2024) — narrative: the name stays, only “(2024)” becomes the number
  • (Ip et al., 2025a) / (… 2025b) — same author & year: use a/b

Rule of thumb: any bracket containing a four-digit year (1900–2099) is treated as a citation.

In the reference list

  • Put it under a heading: References, Reference list, Bibliography or Works cited.
  • One reference per paragraph.
  • Start each entry with the first author’s surname and include the year:
    Smith A, Jones B. Beta trial results. J Delta. 2020.
  • Matching is by surname + year, so those must agree with the in-text citation.

Two references with the same surname & year? List them in order (a, b, …) — the tool maps 2025a to the first and 2025b to the second, and flags them for you to double-check.
